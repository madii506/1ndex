how 1ndex works.

Everything 1ndex does, every address it touches, and how to check all of it yourself.

01what it is

1ndex is the coin that hedges against the stock market. $1NDEX launches on its own launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab. 1% of every trade goes to the index treasury, and the treasury buys four things that tend to hold up or rise when stocks fall: gold, the short Nasdaq, VIX and dollars, a quarter each.

The site compares the four against the S&P 500 live, so you can see on any day whether the hedge is doing its job.

03the rule

  • Members: four tokens on Solana that tend to hold up or rise when stocks fall, each with a real pool on Solana.
  • Weights: a quarter each. Dollars count as a member, so the index is never all-in on the volatile parts.
  • Rebalance: every Monday, 00:00 UTC. The treasury spends what it has collected on whichever parts are under a quarter. It only buys; it never sells into thin pools.
  • The index level on the site starts at 1,000 at the beginning of the 15-day window and moves with the four from equal starting weights.

05the treasury

The treasury is the wallet that created the 1ndex launchpad. LaunchLab sends the launchpad's fee there when it claims, and the same wallet buys the four through Jupiter. Every claim and every buy is a public transaction, and the site reads the wallet's balances straight from the chain.

There is no redeem. $1NDEX is a coin, not a share of the treasury. Holders own a coin whose fees keep buying the hedge.

07the API

Everything on the site comes from these, and you can call them too.

GET/api/ndx?a=basket

the four and the S&P 500: price, 24h change, liquidity, volume and the deepest pool.

GET/api/ndx?a=coin

$1NDEX on its LaunchLab curve: progress, SOL raised, market cap and the fee collected so far.

GET/api/ndx?a=treasury

what the treasury holds, what's waiting to be claimed, and the SOL it has to spend.

GET/api/ndx?a=pad&admin=<wallet>

a wallet's LaunchLab launchpad: fee, LP rule and unclaimed fees.

POST/api/ndx?a=buildPad · buildLaunch · claim

unsigned transactions for the console; they're signed in your own wallet.

08risks

  • A hedge, not a promise. Gold can fall with stocks. The short Nasdaq and VIX funds reset daily and lose value in calm or choppy markets.
  • Thin pools. The VIX fund's pool on Solana is small, so the treasury buys it in small pieces.
  • Issuers keep powers. Tokenized funds are issued by third parties that can pause transfers or move tokens.
  • A coin. $1NDEX can go to zero. The treasury only grows if people trade.
