Rules of thumb, not a forecast: the Nasdaq-100 moves about 1.25× the S&P; the short Nasdaq aims for −3× the Nasdaq; the VIX fund moves about −3.5× the S&P on big days. Gold is held flat.

gold

GLDx tracks the SPDR Gold Shares ETF. Gold doesn't follow stocks: some sell-offs it rises, some it dips.

the short Nasdaq

SQQQ aims for three times the opposite of the Nasdaq-100 each day. It resets daily, so it bleeds in calm or choppy weeks.

VIX

UVXY follows short-term VIX futures, the market's fear gauge. It spikes on crashes and decays fast the rest of the time.

dollars

USDC stays a dollar. It doesn't fall when stocks fall, and it's what the treasury rebalances with.

read this before you buy

  • A hedge, not a promise. Gold can fall with stocks, and the short Nasdaq and VIX lose value in calm or choppy weeks.
  • Thin pools. The VIX fund's pool on Solana is small, so large buys move its price.
  • Issuers keep powers. Tokenized funds are issued by third parties that can pause or move their tokens.
  • A coin, not a share. $1NDEX doesn't redeem for the treasury. It can go to zero.
  • Independent. 1ndex isn't affiliated with Raydium, ProShares, SPDR, Circle or any issuer, and none of this is financial advice.
