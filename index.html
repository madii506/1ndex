the fine print, on-chain

Rules nobody can bend.

01

Your hedge, on-chain

The hedge you pick is written into your coin's metadata link at launch. Anyone can read it, nobody can edit it.

02

1% of every trade

The launchpad fee goes to the hedge treasury. It's set in Raydium LaunchLab, not by this website.

03

No creator fee, no cut

Nobody takes a share of trades besides the hedge and LaunchLab's own 0.25%.

04

Fixed supply

Every coin: 1,000,000,000 tokens, no mint or freeze authority, 793.1M sold on the curve.

05

Half the LP burned

At graduation half the liquidity is burned for good, the other half pays the hedge treasury.

06

Every buy in public

The treasury wallet and every claim and buy show on Solscan, linked from this page.

faq

Questions, answered.

What is 1ndex?

A launchpad on Raydium LaunchLab where every coin comes with a hedge against the stock market. 1% of each coin's trading buys the hedge its creator picked.

What can I pick as a hedge?

Up to four of nine assets: gold (GLDx, PAXG, XAUt0), silver (SLVon), the short Nasdaq (SQQQ), VIX (UVXY), dollars (USDC, USDT) and euros (EURC). Or a ready mix like the classic or the crash shield.

Who holds the hedge?

The 1ndex hedge treasury, one public wallet. It claims the launchpad fees and buys each coin's hedge with that coin's share. Every transaction is on Solscan.

How much does it cost to launch?

Only Solana network fees and rent, about 0.03 SOL. A first buy is optional. 1ndex takes nothing.

Can my hedge change later?

No. It's written into the coin's metadata link when you launch. Launch a new coin for a new hedge.

Does a hedge always rise when stocks fall?

No. Gold can fall with stocks, and the short Nasdaq and VIX lose value in calm weeks. It's a hedge, not a promise.

Is my coin a share of its hedge?

No. A coin doesn't redeem for anything, and its price is set by its own trading. It can go to zero.

Where do the prices come from?

Dexscreener for live prices, GeckoTerminal for history, and Raydium's launch index for the coins on 1ndex.
