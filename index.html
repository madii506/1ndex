faq

Questions, answered.

What is 1ndex?

A coin on Raydium LaunchLab whose 1% trading fee buys four things that tend to hold up or rise when stocks fall: gold, the short Nasdaq, VIX and dollars.

Why these four?

Gold doesn't follow stocks. The short Nasdaq aims for −3× the Nasdaq-100 each day. VIX spikes when stocks crash. Dollars don't fall when stocks fall.

Where does the 1% go?

To the index treasury. It claims the fees from LaunchLab's vault and buys whichever of the four is under a quarter.

Is $1NDEX a share of the treasury?

No. It's a coin, not a fund. It doesn't redeem for the four, and it can go to zero.

Does it always rise when stocks fall?

No. Gold can fall with stocks, and the short Nasdaq and VIX lose value in calm or choppy weeks. It's a hedge, not a promise.

When does it rebalance?

Every Monday, back to a quarter each. The countdown is in the live window at the top of this page.

Where do the prices come from?

Dexscreener for live prices, GeckoTerminal for 4-hour history, all from tokens trading on Solana.

Who holds the treasury?

The wallet that made the launchpad. Its address and every transaction show on this page once $1NDEX launches.

Is it affiliated with Raydium or the issuers?

No. 1ndex is independent and not affiliated with Raydium, ProShares, SPDR, Circle or any issuer.

What can go wrong?

Thin pools move on big buys, issuers can pause their tokens, and the coin's price is set by its own trading. Not financial advice.
